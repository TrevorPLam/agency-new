# Security Policy

## Reporting Security Vulnerabilities

We take security seriously. If you discover a security vulnerability in the Agency Platform Monorepo, please report it to us promptly.

### How to Report
- **Email:** Send details to security@agencyplatform.com (replace with actual contact if available).
- **Include:** A clear description of the vulnerability, steps to reproduce, potential impact, and any suggested fixes.
- **Confidentiality:** We will treat your report confidentially and not disclose it without your permission.

### Response Process
- **Acknowledgment:** We will acknowledge receipt of your report within 48 hours.
- **Investigation:** We will investigate and provide a detailed response within 7 days.
- **Resolution:** We aim to resolve critical vulnerabilities within 30 days, depending on complexity.

### Scope
This policy applies to the Agency Platform Monorepo and its associated packages.

Thank you for helping keep our project secure.
